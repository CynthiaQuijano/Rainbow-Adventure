The Rainbow Returns 🌈

Illustration created by me using Adobe Illustrator.

Rosie continued floating across the beautiful sky, determined to find her missing rainbow colors. Along the way, she remembered all the wonderful places she had visited during her adventure.

Suddenly, colorful sparkles began appearing all around her. The colors slowly came together, forming a beautiful rainbow that stretched across the bright blue sky.

Rosie smiled as her rainbow finally returned. She realized that kindness, courage, and believing in herself made her adventure special. With her colors restored, Rosie happily returned home.
