Rosie floated into a magical forest filled with tall trees, colorful flowers, and sparkling butterflies. She hoped to find one of her missing rainbow colors. Suddenly, Rosie heard a tiny voice crying. Sitting beneath a tall tree was a little bunny named Benny. Benny had wandered too far from home and could not find his family.

"I'm scared," Benny whispered. "I don't know how to get home." Rosie felt sorry for Benny. She wanted to find her missing colors, but she also knew that Benny needed her help.
